# Housing Credit Constraints and Housing Cost Burden of Low-Income Urban Households in Vietnam

> **Status: working project, not peer reviewed.** Results are preliminary and are not summarised here; see the manuscripts in the repository.

Master's thesis topic registration (UEH): how housing-credit constraints relate to the housing cost burden of urban households in Vietnam, with attention to the "missing middle" of low-income households. Contents: LaTeX proposal and manuscript (`01. de cuong & bai viet`), Stata code for the Heckman selection specifications (`10. Bai nop/01. code stata`), variable dictionary and logs.

**Author:** Truong Nhat Duy (Truong Nhat Duy), University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City (UEH).

**Period:** Dec 2025 - Apr 2026 (master's thesis topic registration, UEH). Dates are taken from file timestamps.

## Data availability

Household survey microdata (`.dta`) are excluded (data-use terms of the General Statistics Office). Results are preliminary and not reproduced in this README. Replicating the analysis requires obtaining the original data from the providers.
